About Hospital Y

Our mission

To provide safe, compassionate and affordable specialist care, supported by technology that makes services easy to reach for every patient.

History

Founded in 2005 as a 50-bed clinic, Hospital Y has grown into a 400-bed general hospital with eight specialties and a 24/7 emergency department.

Leadership

Board of Directors chaired by Prof. Nguyen Van Minh; Medical Director Dr. Tran Quang Huy; Head of Nursing Ms. Le Thi Hoa.

Accreditations

Quality management certified to ISO 9001 (illustrative content for this prototype).

Our values

  • Patient safety first
  • Privacy and trust
  • Respect and accessibility for all
